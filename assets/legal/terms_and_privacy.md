## SECTION 2 — PRIVACY POLICY & GLOBAL DATA PROTECTION (GDPR / INTERNATIONAL)

### 2.1 Information Architecture & Local-First Security
VitaCase operates on a **local-first, privacy-by-design** model:
* **Local Vault & On-Device Storage**: Your personal documents, health notes, appointments, medications, vital health logs, and profile entries are stored locally on your mobile device. LIMANEX does not operate remote user accounts, remote user profiles, or cloud document servers for user files.
* **On-Device Authenticated Encryption**: Files in the VitaCase document vault are encrypted locally using authenticated **AES-256-GCM** encryption, with cryptographic keys managed through device hardware-backed secure storage (`FlutterSecureStorage` / Android Keystore / iOS Keychain). Local encryption protects vault files at rest on the device.
* **Off-Device Telemetry Distinction**: While user documents and personal health records remain locally stored on your device, limited technical diagnostics and advertising requests may be transmitted off-device via secure TLS connections by integrated third-party SDKs as detailed in Section 2.4.

### 2.2 On-Device Sensitive Health Data
VitaCase enables you to store health records, medications, and appointment details:
* This information is entered voluntarily by you and stays **only on your device** in encrypted local storage.
* We do **not** transmit, inspect, analyze, monetize, profile, or share your medical files or personal records.
* You are the sole custodian of your health information.

### 2.3 Biometric Authentication
* VitaCase allows you to secure the App using device biometrics (fingerprint or facial recognition).
* Biometric authentication is executed entirely by your device operating system via local platform security APIs (`local_auth`).
* **VitaCase never receives, stores, reads, or transmits biometric templates**. The operating system only returns a local cryptographic status indicating authentication success or failure.

### 2.4 Automatically Collected Technical Data & SDK Telemetry
While your documents and personal records never leave your device, the App integrates vetted third-party software development kits (SDKs) strictly for error monitoring, performance diagnostics, aggregated product analytics, and advertising:

1. **Google Firebase Analytics (Google LLC)**:
   * **Collected Data**: Pseudonymous, aggregated interaction metrics (such as screen visits and feature usage counts).
   * **Safeguard**: No health data, medication names, dosages, or document file contents are ever logged.
2. **Google Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC)**:
   * **Collected Data**: Diagnostic stack traces, device model, operating system version, and system state at the time of a software crash.
   * **Purpose**: Identifying and resolving software stability issues.
3. **Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.)**:
   * **Collected Data**: Real-time application exceptions, error stack traces, and performance events.
   * **User Identifier**: Sentry and Crashlytics receive a randomly generated, installation-specific UUID (`app_user_uid`) stored in secure storage to correlate error occurrences without personal identity.
4. **Google AdMob & Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) (Google LLC)**:
   * **Collected Data**: Mobile advertising identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID), coarse location, device identifiers, and ad interaction events to deliver in-app advertisements.
   * **Consent Handling**: In jurisdictions requiring consent (such as the EEA, UK, and Switzerland), consent preferences for personalized advertising are collected via the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). Advertising consent through UMP is specific to advertising and does not conflate with diagnostic or local processing.

Data collected by their SDKs is transferred using recognized international transfer mechanisms, including the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), ensuring an adequate level of data protection under applicable data-protection regulations.

### 5.3 Limitations: Third-Party Telemetry & Technical Logs
While the in-app "Wipe All Data" feature and app uninstallation completely purge all local user data and documents from your device, **they do not automatically delete telemetry and technical logs that may have been transmitted to third-party services**:
* **Google Firebase (Analytics & Crashlytics)**: Aggregated usage statistics and anonymized crash traces transmitted to Google servers are retained in accordance with Google's standard retention cycles (typically 2 to 14 months for analytics, 90 days for crash logs). The in-app wipe cannot reach or delete records residing on Google servers.
* **Sentry**: Real-time error diagnostic logs sent to Sentry are retained in accordance with Sentry's data retention policy (typically 30 to 90 days) and are not automatically erased by a local device wipe.
* **Google AdMob**: Device-level advertising interactions and frequency capping records managed by Google Mobile Ads SDK follow Google's advertising policies. Users can reset their Advertising Identifier or revoke consent via device system settings or in-app consent options.
