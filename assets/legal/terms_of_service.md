## 2. DESCRIPTION OF SERVICE

VitaCase is a private, offline-first personal organization application that operates on your mobile device. Key features include:

* **Encrypted Document Vault**: On-device storage and organization of personal files, medical documents, prescriptions, lab results, and invoices using local AES-256-GCM encryption.
* **Medication & Inventory Tracking**: Personal medication records, dosage schedules, stock counts, and local reminders.
* **Appointment Scheduling**: Local calendar tracking for doctor visits, examinations, and medical follow-ups.
* **Health & Physical Metrics**: Local tracking of height, weight, BMI, and vital health logs.
* **Emergency & Critical Info**: Quick access to blood type, emergency contact numbers, and important health notes.

VitaCase functions strictly as a **personal organization and local storage tool**.
